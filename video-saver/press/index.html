About Vidkiva

Short description

Vidkiva is an iPhone and iPad app for organizing saved videos and links. Import supported files, create collections, and keep timestamped notes so important moments are easy to revisit.

Extended description

Vidkiva helps you turn scattered video links and files into an organized personal library. Save links to revisit later, import local or authorized videos, organize your collection into folders, and watch locally saved clips with flexible playback controls. Timestamped notes make it easy to return to specific explanations and memorable moments. The app does not require an account for local organization. YouTube videos are link-only with embedded playback where available; YouTube downloading is not supported. Download capabilities depend on the source and your rights to the content.
