Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Video Saver handles information in the app and on this website. It must be reviewed against the final app build and all integrated SDKs before release.

Effective October 31, 2026.

1. Content you choose to save

Video Saver helps you store video URLs, organize collections and manage downloaded media. Links and media may contain personal information. Content you choose to keep is managed in the app’s local library unless you explicitly export, share or use an operating-system storage or backup service.

2. Network requests and third-party sources

When you open or download a video from a third-party source, network requests may be sent to that source or technical services required to retrieve the content. Those third parties may process your IP address, request metadata and other information under their own privacy terms. We do not control third-party websites.

3. Permissions

The app may request access to Photos or Files when you choose to import, save or export media. You can manage these permissions in device settings. The app should not require access to your entire photo library for features that work with files you explicitly select.

4. Analytics and diagnostics

The final release’s analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifiers and other SDK practices have not yet been verified for this policy. No claim of zero tracking or zero data collection is made here. Before public release, this section and the App Store privacy disclosure must accurately list any applicable SDKs, data categories, purposes and sharing.

5. Data retention and deletion

You can delete links, collections and downloaded files through app controls. Deleting the app generally removes app-local data, subject to device backups, exported files and any data stored by third parties. Source websites and external storage providers control their own retention.

6. Website and contact

This product website is hosted as a static website. Its hosting provider and network infrastructure may process technical connection data. If you email us, we use your message and email address to answer your inquiry. For privacy questions, contact jet659521@gmail.com.

7. Children, security and changes

The app is not directed specifically at children. Protect your device and review third-party links before opening them. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure. If our practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date.
