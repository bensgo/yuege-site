Questions

Can I download YouTube videos?

No. Vidkiva saves YouTube links where available and uses embedded playback; it does not download YouTube videos.

Can I watch videos offline?

Local files and successfully downloaded, authorized videos can be played offline. A saved link may still need an internet connection.

Do I need an account?

No account is required to organize your local video library.

Is it available on Android?

The Android version is being prepared. Download links will appear only after it is published.
