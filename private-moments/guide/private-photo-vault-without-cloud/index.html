A private photo vault without cloud uploads

If you prefer to keep sensitive photos on your own iPhone, a local-first vault can reduce dependence on accounts and cloud storage.

What local-first means

Hide Photos: Secret Album keeps vault media and thumbnails on the device and encrypts stored content. The app does not require you to create an account to use the vault.

Where your original photos still live

Importing media into a local vault doesn't remove existing copies from the iOS Photos library or iCloud Photos. Review originals, synced devices, albums, and backups separately.

PIN, Face ID, and auto-lock

A dedicated six-digit PIN helps separate vault access from ordinary gallery browsing. Face ID can make repeated unlocking easier; automatic locking helps when you switch away from the app.

What happens when you change devices

Local storage changes your backup responsibility. Do not assume that a local-only vault automatically transfers encrypted media to a replacement iPhone; check supported export and recovery options before relying on it for irreplaceable photos. See the encrypted vault transfer checklist before replacing your phone.

Questions to ask any photo vault developer

Does it require an account? Does it upload photos? What is encrypted? What analytics does it collect? What happens after reinstalling or forgetting the PIN? Read the product's security and privacy documentation rather than relying on a marketing badge.

Frequently asked questions

Does Hide Photos upload my vault photos to its own server?

No. Hide Photos stores encrypted vault contents locally and does not run a developer-hosted photo storage cloud. If you save an exported backup to another provider, that provider's policies apply. The app may still use limited non-media analytics as described in its privacy policy.

Are my originals removed from Apple Photos after importing?

No, not automatically. Importing creates a separate vault copy. Check Apple Photos, iCloud Photos, Recently Deleted and your other backups before deciding whether to delete originals.

Can I lock albums separately and change their order?

Newer app versions include an individual album Set Passcode option, custom sorting and pin-to-top controls. See the real screenshots above, and check your installed version and plan.

Can Hide Photos import large batches without freezing?

The app has optimized photo and video imports using concurrent encryption work to keep the interface responsive. Results depend on your device, file sizes, available storage and iCloud download status; no universal speed guarantee is made.

What if I lose or change my iPhone?

Create and verify an encrypted backup before deleting the app or replacing the device. Keep the backup file and PIN safely; the developer cannot restore a lost encrypted backup for you. See the transfer guide.
