Photo Vault Without Account or Cloud Uploads on iPhone

A photo vault can store selected media locally without a developer account or a developer-hosted photo cloud. That does not automatically remove Apple Photos originals, disable device backups, or mean the app has no analytics. Here is how to manage those boundaries.

Written by the Hide Photos developer · Yuege Apps

Is a no-account photo vault right for you?

The best choice depends on whether you need a separate PIN, encrypted local storage, a decoy vault, or a backup you can restore yourself. Hide Photos: Secret Album is one option for iPhone users who want an account-free, locally encrypted vault; Apple’s built-in Hidden album may be sufficient for users who prefer fewer apps.

Hide Photos stores vault media and thumbnails on the device, encrypts stored content with AES-GCM, and does not require an account. Consider the limitations of local storage: you must plan for backups, and importing does not automatically erase originals from Apple Photos or iCloud.

Where your original photos still live

Importing media into a local vault doesn't remove existing copies from the iOS Photos library or iCloud Photos. Review originals, synced devices, albums, and backups separately.

PIN, Face ID, and auto-lock

A dedicated vault PIN helps separate vault access from ordinary gallery browsing. Optional Face ID can make repeated unlocking easier when compatible with your vault settings; it is unavailable while a decoy vault is enabled. Automatic locking helps when you switch away from the app.

What happens when you change devices

Local storage changes your backup responsibility. Do not assume that a local-only vault automatically transfers encrypted media to a replacement iPhone; check supported export and recovery options before relying on it for irreplaceable photos. See the encrypted vault transfer checklist before replacing your phone.

Questions to ask any photo vault developer

Does it require an account? Does it upload photos? What is encrypted? What analytics does it collect? What happens after reinstalling or forgetting the PIN? Read the product's security and privacy documentation rather than relying on a marketing badge.

What “no account” and “no cloud uploads” mean

You can create and use the local vault without registering an email address or opening a developer account. Vault photos and videos are not uploaded to developer-operated storage. This is different from saying the entire app never communicates with the internet: the current privacy policy discloses limited Firebase Analytics for app activity and quality measurement.

No vault photos, videos, thumbnails, PINs or keys are sent as analytics events. The policy also excludes photo/video import actions, imported item counts and vault inventory statistics. App Store downloads, purchases, cloud-only source files and a file provider you choose can still involve network access.

How to keep a local library without signing up

  1. Install the app and create a separate vault PIN. An Apple Account used for App Store downloads or purchases is different from an account with the vault developer.
  2. Import a few files already stored on your iPhone, or use the built-in private camera. If an original exists only in iCloud Photos, download it before expecting an offline import.
  3. Lock and reopen the vault, then check the files. Importing makes a vault copy; it does not by itself remove the originals from Apple Photos or other services.
  4. Create an encrypted backup and save it to a location you control. To avoid cloud storage for that export, choose a local location or supported external storage instead of a cloud file provider.
  5. Review your iPhone backup settings and the destinations you use when sharing. The absence of a developer photo cloud does not describe every Apple device-backup or third-party copy.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use it without an email address?

The vault does not require a developer account or email registration. App Store installation and purchases follow Apple’s own account requirements.

Does no account mean no analytics?

No. Account registration, vault uploads and analytics are separate issues. This app discloses limited Firebase Analytics; read the privacy policy for the specific exclusions and configuration.

Will my photos still be in iCloud?

Imported originals can remain in Apple Photos and iCloud Photos. Exported backups can also be in iCloud Drive if you choose that destination. Review each copy and your device-backup settings separately.

Do I need a cloud storage subscription?

A local vault does not require subscribing to a developer photo cloud. It uses device storage, and some app features require Pro. You still need a backup plan that fits the space available to you.

Can support restore my photos after I lose my phone?

There is no developer-hosted vault library to download again. Keep a compatible encrypted backup and the credentials needed to restore it. No account also means no account-based media recovery.
