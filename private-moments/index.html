Private photo vault for iPhone

Keep private photos, videos and moments under your control.

Hide Photos: Secret Album keeps private photos, videos, notes and personal moments in an on-device encrypted vault, protected by a 6-digit PIN and optional Face ID. Organize media with individual album locks, custom sorting and pin-to-top controls, and use up to three decoy vaults for separate private spaces.

Import multiple photos and videos with an interface designed to stay responsive, and create encrypted backup files to move your vault to another iPhone without a Hide Photos cloud account.
