Privacy Policy

Your vault is not our data.

Hide Photos: Secret Album is designed to keep private content on your device and outside developer-operated servers.

Effective date: October 21, 2026. This policy applies to the Hide Photos: Secret Album iOS app and this product website.

1. Information we collect

Hide Photos: Secret Album does not require an account and does not include advertising. We use Firebase Analytics for limited app activity and quality measurement. We do not send photo or video import actions, imported item counts, the number of photos or videos stored in your vault, or vault inventory statistics as analytics events. We also do not send photos, videos, thumbnails, Moment text, PINs, encryption keys, or other vault content as analytics events.

Analytics is configured without IDFA, without AdSupport, with IDFV collection disabled, without Google Signals, and without advertising-related features.

2. Your private content

Private content is stored inside the app's private container on your iPhone and is protected locally. Hide Photos: Secret Album does not operate a server that stores your vault content.

3. Photos and camera access

If you choose to import from Apple Photos, Hide Photos: Secret Album uses the permissions you grant through iOS to access the selected content. If you use the private camera, camera and microphone access may be requested for photo or video capture. Media captured with the private camera is designed to go directly into the Hide Photos: Secret Album vault rather than being automatically added to Apple Photos.

4. Encrypted backups

Backups are created at your request and remain under your control. You choose where to save them, such as Files, iCloud Drive, external storage, or another file provider available through iOS. Hide Photos: Secret Album does not upload a copy to a developer-operated backup service.

Storage providers you choose may process the backup according to their own privacy policies. The backup itself is encrypted by Hide Photos: Secret Album before export.

5. Sharing and exporting

When you explicitly export or share content, iOS may hand that content to the destination you select. That action is initiated by you, and the selected app or service may process the content under its own privacy policy.

6. Analytics and advertising

Firebase Analytics is used for limited app activity and quality measurement. It does not track photo or video imports or the quantities of media stored in the vault. Hide Photos: Secret Album does not serve ads and does not use analytics for advertising personalization. Technical data processed by Firebase is handled according to Google's Firebase privacy and data-processing documentation.

7. Website data

This website is hosted as a static site. Hide Photos: Secret Album does not intentionally add advertising trackers or behavioral analytics to these pages. Your network provider, browser, DNS provider, hosting platform, or other infrastructure providers may still process technical connection data as part of delivering the site.

8. Data retention

Because Hide Photos: Secret Album does not collect your vault content on developer-operated servers, we do not maintain a server-side copy to retain or delete. Content remains on your device until you remove it, uninstall the app, or otherwise manage it through iOS and your own backups.

9. Children

Hide Photos: Secret Album is a general-purpose privacy utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require account registration.

10. Security

Hide Photos: Secret Album uses local encryption and iOS platform protections to reduce exposure of private data. No software can truthfully guarantee absolute security. You can read the current technical overview on the Security page.

11. Changes to this policy

If Hide Photos: Secret Album's data practices materially change, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes. The effective date at the top of this page will also be updated.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, Hide Photos: Secret Album's privacy practices, or need product support, contact the developer at jet659521@gmail.com.

You can also visit the Hide Photos: Secret Album Support page for troubleshooting and common questions.
