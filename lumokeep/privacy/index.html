Privacy Policy

Privacy by design.

LumoKeep is designed to help you organize and clean up your photo library while keeping your personal media under your control.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how LumoKeep handles information when you use the app.

2. Photos and Videos

LumoKeep requests access to your photo library so you can review, organize, clean up, and compress photos and videos.

Your photos and videos are processed on your device for LumoKeep’s core organization and cleanup features. LumoKeep does not require you to create an account to use the app.

3. Local App Data

LumoKeep may store app preferences, organization state, cleanup decisions, progress, and other app-related data locally on your device so the app can remember your choices and provide its features.

4. Photo and Video Compression

When you use photo or video compression, the selected media is processed on your device. LumoKeep does not operate a cloud service for storing your personal photo library.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

If you purchase LumoKeep Pro or another in-app purchase, payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. LumoKeep does not receive your full payment card details.

6. Analytics and Advertising

LumoKeep does not use your personal photos or videos for advertising. The app is designed to provide its core photo organization and cleanup features without requiring an account.

7. Permissions

LumoKeep only requests permissions that are needed for the features you choose to use, such as access to your photo library. You can manage permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

8. Children

LumoKeep is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through an account-based service.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when LumoKeep’s features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
