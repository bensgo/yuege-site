Similar vs Duplicate iPhone Photos: What Should You Keep?

Similar-looking photos may differ in focus, expression, timing and framing. Apple Photos has a Duplicates collection on supported iOS versions, but it does not represent every similar photo. Use LumoKeep to review similar shots and confirm choices before deletion.

Practical steps

  1. Check which photos and videos occupy storage.
  2. Review results inside LumoKeep before confirming.
  3. Keep backups of important originals.

If iCloud Photos is enabled, deleting items may sync across devices. File-size savings vary.
