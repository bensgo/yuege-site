Choosing an ad-free photo cleaner for iPhone

An ad-free photo cleaner should reduce distraction without taking control of your photo library. Here are the features worth checking before installing one.

Look for clear keep-or-delete decisions

A trustworthy cleanup flow shows what you have selected for deletion. LumoKeep uses deliberate swipe review followed by a final confirmation step.

Check the free tier and optional upgrades

A free download does not always mean every feature is free. LumoKeep provides core photo cleanup functionality free, with some advanced capabilities offered through Pro.

Compare similar photos carefully

Near-identical pictures are not necessarily duplicates. Review differences in focus, framing and expressions, rather than automatically removing every similar-looking shot.

Understand where processing happens

Photo libraries can contain sensitive images and documents. LumoKeep is designed for on-device organization and processing without an account requirement. Read its privacy policy before granting photo access.

Confirm what 'no ads' means

LumoKeep does not include in-app advertising. It may still offer an optional Pro upgrade, which is separate from third-party ads.
