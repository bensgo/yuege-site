How to Find Large Photos and Videos on iPhone

Check Settings → General → iPhone Storage. Then use LumoKeep's Large Files feature to review larger photos and videos. Large files are not necessarily unnecessary. Back up important originals and review before deletion. Recently Deleted and iCloud syncing may delay space savings.

Practical steps

  1. Check which photos and videos occupy storage.
  2. Review results inside LumoKeep before confirming.
  3. Keep backups of important originals.

If iCloud Photos is enabled, deleting items may sync across devices. File-size savings vary.
