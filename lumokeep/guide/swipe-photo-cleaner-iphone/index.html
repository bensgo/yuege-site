How to clean up iPhone photos by swiping

When your camera roll gets crowded, deciding what to keep is often harder than finding photos. A swipe-based review makes that decision manageable, one picture at a time.

Start with a review, not an automatic deletion

Open your photo library in LumoKeep and work through photos individually. Mark shots you want to keep and candidates you no longer need. A quick review is useful, but you make the final decision.

Check the delete selection before confirming

Review marked photos before removing them. Look again at screenshots containing receipts, document scans, favorite expressions and other images that can be difficult to replace.

Similar photos need judgment

Two photos of the same scene may differ in sharpness or expression. Similar images are not necessarily exact duplicates; compare them before choosing what to remove.

What if iCloud Photos is enabled?

Deletion in Apple's photo library may synchronize to other devices using the same iCloud Photos library. Confirm important originals and backups first.

Who benefits from swipe cleanup?

This approach is helpful when you want a simple, hands-on way to reduce clutter rather than an app that makes deletion decisions automatically.

Questions people ask

Is swipe-based photo cleaning automatic?

No. In LumoKeep, swiping helps you classify photos and review what you selected before deletion.

Can I keep important photos?

Yes. The workflow is designed to let you decide which pictures to keep. Always check the final deletion selection.
