How to find similar photos on iPhone

Your camera roll can contain near-identical photos from bursts, trips, events and retakes. Similar is not always the same as duplicate.

Try Apple's Duplicates utility first

In supported iOS versions, the Photos app may show a Duplicates collection under Utilities when it identifies duplicate items. If you don't see it, there may be no eligible duplicates or the library may still be processing.

Similar photos require judgment

A burst can contain subtly different framing, expressions, or focus. Algorithms can help group comparable shots, but you should decide which images to keep.

Look for large but important originals

Live Photos, edited photos, and different resolutions can behave differently. Avoid deleting the highest-quality original without checking details and backup status.

Review similar shots in LumoKeep

LumoKeep helps surface similar photos so you can compare groups and choose the images worth keeping. It also offers swipe-based review, screenshots, and large-file cleanup.

Make deletion a separate decision

After choosing unwanted photos, review the selected set one more time. On devices using iCloud Photos, deletions can propagate across synchronized libraries.

Note: iOS menus and behavior may change across versions. Confirm the options shown on your device.
