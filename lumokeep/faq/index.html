LumoKeep FAQ: Free iPhone Photo Cleaner, Swipe Review & Privacy

Answers about LumoKeep, the ad-free iPhone photo cleaner: free features, swipe review, similar photos, local processing and safe deletion.

What is LumoKeep?

LumoKeep is an iPhone photo cleaner and organizer that helps you swipe through photos, review similar shots, sort screenshots, identify large media, and decide what to remove. It emphasizes reviewing choices before deletion.

Is LumoKeep free to download?

Yes. LumoKeep is free to download and offers core photo cleanup features at no cost. Some advanced tools may require Pro.

Does LumoKeep show ads?

LumoKeep is designed without in-app advertising.

Does LumoKeep automatically delete my photos?

No. LumoKeep is built around user-controlled cleanup. You review and confirm selected items before deletion.

Can LumoKeep find similar photos and exact duplicates?

LumoKeep helps you review similar shots. Similar-looking photos are not always byte-for-byte duplicates; compare focus, expressions and details before removing any image.

Does LumoKeep upload my photo library?

LumoKeep is designed for on-device photo organization and processing without requiring an account. See the privacy policy for full details.

Can I clean up photos by swiping left or right?

Yes. The swipe review workflow lets you make keep-or-remove decisions one photo at a time and review your selections before deletion.

Does deleting photos free up iPhone storage immediately?

Not always. Items can remain in Recently Deleted, and storage reporting or iCloud Photos synchronization can take time. Confirm backups before deleting originals.

Is LumoKeep available on Android?

LumoKeep is an iOS app. An Android version is not currently advertised.

How is LumoKeep different from one-tap photo cleaners?

LumoKeep prioritizes manual review and informed cleanup decisions over automatically deleting images you may want to keep.
